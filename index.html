How it works

Everything in one place,
done at your own pace

1

Create your account

Simply your name, email and a password. Nothing more. No forms, no fuss.

We will never send you promotional emails, newsletters or advertisements. The only emails you will receive are updates about your plan.

2

Plan the funeral

We guide you gently through each detail — music, flowers, readings and more. Save and return whenever you're ready.

3

Send to directors

When you're ready, enter your postcode and choose local funeral directors to receive your plan.

4

They come to you

Local funeral directors receive your plan and will be in touch to discuss the final arrangements. You arrive prepared — they can focus on giving your loved one the send off they deserve.
